Site Name

🧀 Jakes LegenDairy Cheese

  • A website about Jakes Legendairy Cheese.
  • Jakes Legendairy Cheese is a brand that I have used to promote and sell cheese that I personally make and have sold at a farmers’ market.
  • jakeslegendairycheese.com available for purchase

Site Purpose

The website serves as an online presence for Jakes LegenDairy Cheese. It also should provide the following:

  • The cheese making process (from milk to wheel)
  • Product catalog with flavor profiles
  • Recipes suggestions
  • Farm stories from our family
  • Market schedule and contact information
  • Educational articles about cheese

Scenarios

Scenarios are questions that are asked by a site visitor who represents the target audience.

Scenario 1

Question: "How is Jakes Legendairy Cheese made?"

Answer provided by site: Detailed step-by-step cheese making process with photos.

Scenario 2

Question: "What cheeses are available right now and what do they taste like?"

Answer provided by site: Dynamic product grid with flavor notes, aging details, and seasonal availability.

Scenario 3

Question: "Where can I buy Jakes cheese or visit him at the farmers market?"

Answer provided by site: Market schedule, online order form, and contact information.
